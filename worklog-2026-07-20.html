Stretch 1 — allocation pass · mature runs · audit · visualization

  • Allocation-reduction pass a2b26dc — in-place reaction evaluation plus a hoisted lightFactor allocation: 616 → 521 KB/step (~15%), bit-identical. Deliberately stopped short of reusing Solver A's sparse factorization — it touches the linear solve and needs a forced fixed-pattern matrix (the Cahn-Hilliard mobility zeroes entries, so sparse() drops them).   • Mature-filter runs — clean-start and seeded-mature. Surfaced a real constraint: osmosis drives the enclosed fraction to β/(1−β) ≈ 99× the attached fraction, so a mature seed must keep the attached fraction below ~1% or it clogs instantly. Confirmed the physics — attachment is confined to the sand, and the schmutzdecke builds from the surface downward.
  • Mass-conservation audit — with reactions off, biomass balances against integrated boundary fluxes to 8.5×10⁻⁹. The residual is integration error, not a leak (the fluxes telescope). This was the check needed before trusting longer runs.

Findings

What the day established

Attachment/settling works and is confined to the sand. The ~0.14-day transit time explains why every earlier short test showed only transport.
The model does not auto-clog — it self-limits at φb ≈ 0.63; clogging is driven by the attachment/growth parameters.
sand_pathogen (default 0) is the dominant pathogen-removal knob — worth foregrounding in the paper's pathogen-removal discussion.
The temperature correction is very weak. The ratio form θ^(T_K/293 − 1) gives ~0.2% change per 10 °C, versus the authoritative θ^(293 − T_K). For a paper about variable environmental conditions, this needs resolving.

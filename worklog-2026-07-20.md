### Stretch 1 — allocation pass · mature runs · audit · visualization

- **Allocation-reduction pass** (`a2b26dc`) — converted the reaction evaluation
  to an in-place kernel and hoisted the per-step `lightFactor` allocation out of
  the loop: **616 → 521 KB/step (~15%)**, bit-identical (all four golden masters
  unchanged). I deliberately *stopped short* of the bigger win — reusing Solver
  A's sparse factorization — because it touches the linear solve and needs a
  forced fixed-pattern matrix (the Cahn-Hilliard mobility zeroes entries, so
  `sparse()` drops them and the pattern isn't stable for `lu!` reuse). Left for a
  supervised session.

- **Mature-filter runs** — a clean-start run and a seeded-mature run. Surfaced a
  real physical constraint: the osmosis relaxation drives the enclosed volume
  fraction to **β/(1−β) ≈ 99×** the attached fraction, so my first mature seed
  clogged instantly. A valid mature biofilm needs attached fraction below ~1%
  with the enclosed water seeded near osmotic equilibrium. Confirmed the physics:
  **attachment is confined to the sand** (zero in the water column), and the
  schmutzdecke builds from the surface downward, ripening deeper over time.

- **Mass-conservation audit** — with reactions off, total biomass balances
  against integrated boundary fluxes to **8.5×10⁻⁹ relative** over 99 steps. The
  residual is integration error, not a leak (the upwind fluxes telescope, so the
  scheme is conservative by construction). This was the check needed before
  trusting longer runs.

- **HTML artifact** — depth–time heatmaps of the filter column, ripening depth
  profiles, the suspended→attached mass budget, and the audit, with honest
  caveats built in (the 0.3-day runs are transient, not steady-state removal).
  Published privately: <https://claude.ai/code/artifact/d9d11444-7615-4427-9e0e-46e3ebf6a176>

### Stretch 2 — sensitivity analysis + long-term run

- **Long-term background run** — 20 simulated days (1M steps, 55 s wall). Result:
  **flag OK, does not clog.** The filter self-limits to max φb ≈ 0.63
  (osmosis-dominated) and reaches a quasi-steady mature biofilm by ~0.3 d. So
  clogging in this model is **parameter-dependent, not automatic**.

- **Sensitivity analysis** (`a1cf2a8`) — a reusable one-at-a-time (OAT) harness at

## Key findings

- **Attachment/settling works and is confined to the sand**; the ~0.14-day
  transit time explains why every earlier (short) test showed only transport.
- **The model does not auto-clog** — it self-limits at φb ≈ 0.63; clogging is
  driven by the attachment/growth parameters.
- **`sand_pathogen` (default 0) is the dominant pathogen-removal knob.** Worth
  foregrounding in the paper's pathogen-removal discussion.
- **The temperature correction is very weak.** The Julia/MPC-SSF ratio form
  `θ^(T_K/293 − 1)` gives ~0.2% change per 10 °C, versus the authoritative
  slow-sand form `θ^(293 − T_K)`. For a paper about *variable environmental
  conditions*, this formula choice matters and should be resolved.
